THE DEFINING QUESTION

“Knowing what we know today, would we still choose to make the same investment?”
OPUS keeps the question alive. APEX helps an authorised person answer it.

Most organisations measure whether transformation is being delivered.

APEX asks whether it remains worth delivering—and what the evidence really supports.

THE PROBLEM

Delivery can remain busy, governed and apparently on plan—while quietly losing its purpose.

Programmes begin with a business case, expected benefits and a set of assumptions. Then the organisation changes. Leadership, markets, regulation, technology, actors and priorities move on. The programme often continues.

Traditional governance is good at recording activity and approving change. It is much less effective at reasoning across those changes and asking whether the investment still makes sense.

A green status does not prove that a programme is on track. It proves that someone recorded it as green.

APEX is being researched to connect the original purpose, today's evidence and the decision now required.

STRATEGIC DRIFT

Original intent What the organisation chose to achieve
Current delivery What the programme is now building

The programme succeeds. The transformation fails.

ILLUSTRATIVE FUTURE CASE · NOT A RESULT FROM THE CURRENT POC

CASE STUDY 01 · DEMONSTRATED

Should Northstar Release R12 go live?

Northstar Platform Renewal is a controlled fictional programme at Meridian Financial Services plc. Release R12 changes customer servicing, payment resilience, operational monitoring, vulnerable-customer support and rollback capability.

APEX ASSESSMENT Conditionally Ready Unconditional approval is not supported by the current evidence.
CustomerMeridian Financial Services plc

Regulated service owner and final customer authority.

Responsible for delivery of Release R12.

OperationsHarbour Platform Operations Ltd

Independent assuranceVerity Assurance Partners LLP

Independent test and assurance challenge.

THE QUIETEST VOICE

Technical progress was visible. Downstream business readiness was less certain.

The change could affect call volume, handling time, complaints, backlogs, vulnerable customers and operational capacity. The current POC did not measure those effects; it established that they required evidence and accountable business consideration. The Operations Representative was absent from the release decision and the operational checklist remained unsigned.

NINE RULES REQUIRE ACTION

Evidence incomplete

Two mandatory tests, production-like rollback rehearsal and verified deployment credentials.

Business and governance incomplete

Operational risk acceptance, Release Board quorum and a completed operational acceptance checklist.

The decision pack is not current

CCN-012 is missing from the gate baseline, assurance used an older pack and the earlier approval expired when the date changed.
