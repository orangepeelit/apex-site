APEX / Roadmap

ROADMAP

A controlled path from proposition to evidence.

The roadmap describes intended areas of development. It is directional rather than contractual and will change as the research and practical work evolve.

CURRENT

Foundation

Clarify the OPUS methodology, terminology, hierarchy, principles and public narrative.

NEXT

Formalisation

Develop clearer definitions, testable propositions, boundary conditions and an initial working paper.

PLANNED

Knowledge model

Create a small reference ontology and worked organisational examples.

PLANNED

APEX prototype

Implement selected elements of the methodology in a controlled demonstrator.

FUTURE

Applied studies

Use practical contexts to test usefulness, limitations and unintended consequences.

FUTURE

Publication and collaboration

Publish mature work, invite challenge and develop the programme with relevant practitioners and researchers.

ROADMAP DISCIPLINE

No artificial certainty.

Dates and commitments will be introduced only where they are supported by a credible delivery plan. Research findings may change the sequence.
