# Changelog

## v0.5 — Multi-page foundation

### Corrected

- Restored **APEX** as the microsite and practical implementation environment.
- Defined **OPUS** as the methodology.
- Defined the **OPUS research programme** as the work used to develop, test and challenge the methodology.
- Corrected the release number to v0.5.

### Added

- Multi-page site structure:
  - Home
  - OPUS Methodology
  - Research
  - Papers
  - Applications
  - Roadmap
  - About
  - Contact
- Shared navigation and footer across all pages.
- Honest publication status for papers that have not yet been produced.
- Directional roadmap language that avoids unsupported delivery commitments.
- Extended responsive styles for article pages, cards, timelines and publication listings.

### Changed

- Rewrote the home page around organisational knowledge rather than product marketing.
- Preserved the established Orange Peel visual identity, typography, colour palette and reveal animations.
- Repositioned APEX beneath the OPUS methodology without presenting OPUS as a theory or renaming the site.
- Removed draft and publishing-placeholder language.
