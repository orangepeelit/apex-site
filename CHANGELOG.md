# Changelog

## v0.6 — First proof of concept demonstrated

### Added

- The OPUS-to-APEX evolution story.
- A clear distinction between OPUS as the research-led operating system and APEX as the product.
- A visual, animated journey from Actor question through qualification, organisational evidence, ontology, reasoning and decision brief.
- Explicit qualifying questions before APEX produces an answer.
- A definition of the organisation's IQ and the source-qualification controls applied before ingestion.
- A data-driven case-study selector using `case-studies.json`.
- Northstar Release R12 as Case Study 01 rather than the entire product narrative.
- Future case-study questions covering programme confidence, benefits intelligence and strategic drift.
- A controlled illustrative 72% trajectory-divergence example, clearly labelled as not yet demonstrated.
- A guided case-study interview prototype that forms a draft brief and optionally creates an email.
- Full Orange Peel header and footer branding, favicon and touch icon.

### Restored from v0.5

- “Transformation should be continuously justified. Not just continuously delivered.”
- The broader problem of programmes losing purpose while appearing governed and on plan.
- Delivery Intelligence, Benefits Intelligence, Strategic Drift and Explainable Reasoning.
- The defining investment question and Orange Peel principle.
- A British, practical and sceptical voice centred on evidence and accountable judgement.

### Demonstrated

- Fourteen controlled Northstar sources mapped.
- Thirty-six source-to-ontology mappings.
- Twenty-five of twenty-five delivery questions answered.
- Ten of ten seeded contradictions detected.
- Ten readiness rules executed.
- Zero graph-validation violations.
- A **Conditionally Ready** assessment with medium-high confidence.
- Nine unmet conditions, required actions and retained human authorities.

### Changed

- Reframed the site from a Northstar-only narrative to a cumulative OPUS and APEX research story.
- Positioned the conversational interface as the front door to reasoning, not the intelligence itself.
- Explained that APEX asks clarifying questions where context is incomplete or ambiguous.
- Made the APEX Decision Brief the tangible product output.
- Updated the roadmap around a growing library of research-led case studies.

### Next

- Run structured founder interviews to form Case Studies 02 and 03.

- Test benefits and strategic-drift reasoning against controlled fixtures.
- Explore a voice interface over the same qualifying-question and decision-brief pattern.
- Prepare controlled live-pilot conditions.
