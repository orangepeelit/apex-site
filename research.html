THE RESEARCH PROGRAMME

Develop the methodology by trying to expose where it is wrong.

The OPUS research programme exists to investigate, challenge and improve the methodology through evidence, comparison and practical application.

Research standard

The purpose is not to accumulate material that agrees with OPUS. It is to identify competing explanations, boundary conditions, contradictions and situations in which the methodology produces little or no value.

Methods under consideration

Literature review, comparative analysis, formal definition, worked examples, case-based observation, prototype implementation and reflective evaluation.

Research integrity

Claims should distinguish evidence, interpretation and conjecture. Practical examples should protect confidentiality. Unsupported certainty should not be introduced merely to make the work appear complete.

RESEARCH INTENT

Challenge before promotion.

The methodology should endure only while evidence and practical experience continue to support it.
