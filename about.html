ABOUT THE WORK

Developed from practice, tested through research.

APEX and OPUS are being developed by Orange Peel IT Services from experience of complex transformation, programme recovery, delivery assurance and organisational decision-making.

Origin

The work began with repeated delivery situations in which organisations had abundant reporting but lacked a shared, evidence-based understanding of what was happening and why.

That practical problem led to broader questions about organisational memory, evidence, reasoning, decision rights and learning.

Orange Peel IT Services

Orange Peel IT Services is an independent technology consultancy focused on transformation, delivery leadership, programme recovery, assurance, implementation and governance.

OPUS forms part of the consultancy's developing intellectual and delivery methodology. APEX provides a controlled space to explore how that methodology may be implemented through technology.

Position

This is an emerging body of work. It should be judged by the clarity of its definitions, the quality of its evidence, its usefulness in practice and its willingness to change when challenged by reality.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Independent enough to challenge. Practical enough to implement.

The work aims to remain grounded in real organisational problems rather than becoming detached conceptual language.
