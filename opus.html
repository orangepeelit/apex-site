APEX / OPUS

THE METHODOLOGY

OPUS helps organisations reason from reality rather than from fragments.

OPUS is a developing methodology for representing organisational knowledge, distinguishing evidence from assumption, reasoning transparently and learning from outcomes.

Purpose

Organisations generate records, reports, conversations, decisions and experience continuously. Yet these assets rarely form a coherent account of what the organisation knows.

OPUS provides a practical structure for turning dispersed information into explainable organisational understanding without transferring decision-making authority away from people.

Core elements

Ontology
The things that exist in the organisation and the relationships that give them meaning.
Knowledge
What is represented as known, including its source, context and provenance.
Evidence
The observations and records that support or challenge an assertion.
Reasoning
The transparent connection of evidence, relationships and assumptions into conclusions.
Confidence
A visible expression of uncertainty rather than a claim of certainty.
Decision
An accountable choice made by an authorised actor.
Outcome
What reality produces after a decision or action.
Learning
The revision of organisational understanding in response to outcomes and new evidence.

Emerging principles

Reality precedes applications. Evidence precedes reasoning. Assumptions should remain visible. Confidence is not certainty. Decisions belong to actors. Outcomes become new evidence. Reality always wins.

What OPUS is not

OPUS is not a claim that organisations can be reduced to a perfect model. It is not an automated decision authority, a substitute for leadership or a promise that uncertainty can be eliminated.

It is a disciplined way of making knowledge, evidence, assumptions and reasoning more visible so that people can exercise better judgement.

WORKING POSITION

A methodology, not a product claim.

OPUS is expected to evolve. Its language, structures and principles must remain open to challenge from research and practical use.
